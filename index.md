# Marites Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 16, 2026**

Marites ("Marites," "we," "us," or "our") is a community information application operated by an independent individual developer. Marites helps users discover nearby community events, view reports, and share information about local activities and incidents.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and managed when you use Marites.

**Privacy and support contact:** howtomarites@gmail.com

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create a Marites account, we process your email address and information necessary to authenticate your account.

Authentication is provided through Supabase.

### Location Information

With your permission, Marites accesses your device's location to help center the map, discover nearby events, and calculate distances.

When you create a shared event, the location you select on the map is stored as part of that event.

Other users may be able to view the locations of shared community events.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

### Community Reports and Content

When you contribute to Marites, we may process:

- Event titles and descriptions
- Report text and status updates
- Event locations
- Uploaded photographs
- Submission timestamps
- Account identifiers associated with shared content

Shared events and reports may be visible to other authenticated Marites users.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in community submissions.

### Locally Stored Information

Marites also supports events and reports saved locally on your device.

This information is separate from community content published through the shared database.

### Advertising Information

Marites integrates Google AdMob for advertising.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP addresses, device information, advertising identifiers, and advertisement interaction data, subject to applicable privacy choices and device permissions.

## 2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Create and authenticate accounts
- Display nearby community events
- Publish community reports and updates
- Process selected photos
- Provide location-related functionality
- Maintain application reliability and security
- Manage account deletion requests
- Display advertisements when permitted

## 3. Third-Party Services

Marites uses third-party services to support its functionality.

**Supabase** provides authentication, database hosting, backend functions, and file storage.

**Google AdMob** provides advertising services and related privacy-management functionality.

These providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Shared community submissions may also be viewed by other users of Marites.

## 4. Location Privacy

Marites requests location permission to support nearby-event discovery and distance calculations.

You can deny or revoke location permission through iOS Settings.

Some location-related features may be limited without permission.

Locations associated with shared community reports should not be considered private.

## 5. Camera and Photos

Marites may request camera access when you choose to take a photo for a report.

You can also select photos using Apple's system photo picker.

Photos attached to shared reports may be uploaded to Supabase Storage and made available alongside the corresponding report.

## 7. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Marites account within the application.

Navigate to:

**Account → Delete account**

Account deletion requires deliberate confirmation.

The deletion process is designed to remove your authentication account, shared events, and shared reports.

Reports attached to events you created may also be deleted, including reports submitted by other users.

Associated uploaded photos are processed for deletion through direct removal or scheduled cleanup.

Events and reports saved only on your device are not automatically removed when your online account is deleted.

For assistance, contact howtomarites@gmail.com.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information as necessary to operate Marites and support its functionality.

Shared account information and associated content are subject to the account-deletion process described above.

Certain information may be retained when required by applicable law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

Third-party providers may maintain information according to their own applicable retention practices.

## 9. Data Security

Marites uses authentication, database access controls, and server-side authorization measures to help protect user information.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.


## 10. Children's Privacy

Marites is intended for users aged 13 and older.
It is not directed toward children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided
personal information through Marites, please
contact howtomarites@gmail.com so that we can
review the matter and take appropriate action.

Age-screening and additional protections for
teenage users are being developed as part of
Marites' release preparation.
